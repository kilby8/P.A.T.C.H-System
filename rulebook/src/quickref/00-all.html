First SessionZero to first roll in 20 minutes

Players: build an operator

  1. Pick an origin and a background (Handbook Ch. 3).
  2. Every attribute starts at 1; spend 12 points, max 4 each.
  3. Background skills at rank 2, plus 2 free ranks (max 2).
  4. Derived stats: HW 5 + 5×HDW · OS 16 + 2×SYS + DATA · AP 1 + SYS÷2 · Def 10 + PING + armor.
  5. Loadout and blueprints from your background; blueprints go to your stockpile.
  6. Fragments 120 (Volunteer 138); Engagement and viewers from the CLOUT table.
  7. Spend 2 AdvP: one tier-1 node + one of its sub-skills.
  8. One sentence: why are you in the arena?

GM: before the table

  • Open the app's GM tab; create a session code.
  • Pick a sector-1 deployment with 2 encounters.
  • Budget 4 threat per operator, or roll a Spectacle preset.
  • Print one Operator Record per player.

First scene

  • Read the induction memo aloud.
  • Call one easy check (target 10) per player before the first fight.
  • Call one highlight on camera.

Session shape

Cold open → deployment (2–3 fights) → 15-minute table break = Condo Hours → Condo Phase → stinger.

What to print

This PDF: pages 3–4 are the Quick Reference (one double-sided sheet per player) and pages 5–6 are the Operator Record (one double-sided sheet per operator). Keep the Arena Bestiary at the GM's side and the Operator's Handbook on the table.

Quick ReferencePage 1 // Checks & Combat

The core roll

Roll d6 equal to attribute + skill. Sum them. Meet or beat the target. Untrained: attribute only. Points over the target = flair (attacks: +1 damage per full 5; 10+ on camera = +1 Engagement). Points under = shortfall (1–2: success at a cost).

Advantage: roll 1 extra die, drop the lowest. Disadvantage: roll 1 extra, drop the highest. +N dice stack. Saves: attribute + best related skill, against the save target.

Attacking

Weapon attribute + skill against the target's Defense. A hit deals weapon dice + attribute bonus (attr − 3) + flair bonus; minimum 1.

Range: double range with disadvantage. Ranged attacks while in melee: disadvantage. Cover: half +2, heavy +5, full can't be targeted.

Damage & vitals

Damage hits Overshield first, then Hardware. Overshield resets after every encounter. Hardware heals only in the Condo (25% per hour), through Patch Up (2 AP) or through abilities.

Neural Shock: Hardware below 50% → −1 AP.

Flatlined at 0: crash save 1d6 each turn, 4+ succeeds (6 = two successes, 1 = two failures). 3 successes: stable. 3 failures: build destroyed.

Destructive installation

Install in the Condo. Each install consumes the blueprint. Hardpoints: Foundation 1, Tactical 2, Apex 3. Installing over a hardpoint destroys the old blueprint.

Condo Phase

  • Overshield full.
  • 3 Condo Hours: 25% of max HW each (5 real min each on the app timer).
  • Terminal: buy, trade, install, swap.
  • Spend AdvP.
  • One scene each: on air (CLOUT + Performance 15 for +2 Eng.) or dark (GM sets the cost).

Advancement

Start with 2 AdvP; +1 per deployment survived. Node 1 · sub-skill 1 · skill rank 2 · attribute 4. No refunds; the GM can reverse a mistaken sub-skill purchase. Tier 2 needs a tier 1 in its zone; tier 3 needs a tier 2. Overlap nodes need both parent zones.
