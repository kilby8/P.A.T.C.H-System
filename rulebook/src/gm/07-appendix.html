Encounter Checklist

  1. Sector, goal and Spectacle Score.
  2. Budget: 4 threat per operator; spend it.
  3. Terrain: cover, one hazard, one camera moment.
  4. Surprise? (Handbook, Combat: Encounter Flow.) Initiative: operators roll PING, mobs use Init.
  5. Run it. Call highlights. Track Engagement.
  6. End: reset Overshields, pay out, roll sector loot, consider a sponsor drop.

Book & App

The books are generated from the companion app's own rules data, so the tables can't drift apart: skills, backgrounds, derived stats, gear, blueprints, the archetype tree, conditions, threat tiers, the loot table and every stat block. When a rule changes, it changes in the app first, and the next build of these PDFs picks it up.

Rules still marked PLAYTEST are in the app too; the tag means "tune after real sessions", not "not implemented".
