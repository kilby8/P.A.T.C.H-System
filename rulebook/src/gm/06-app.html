The Companion App

The P.A.T.C.H. HUD tracks vitals, AP, gear and the broadcast meter for every operator, and gives you a console to run it all.

In this chapter
Getting it · Shared sessions · GM sign-in · Console actions mapped to the rules · What it doesn't do yet

Getting the App

Open patch-system.pages.dev on any device. Use it in the browser, or install it to the home screen from patch-system.pages.dev/install. An Android APK is also on the install page. Once installed, it opens without signal.

Shared sessions

Every device that joins the same session code sees the same party live. Players log in as their operator, and the GM logs in on the GM tab.

GM sign-in

GM access needs a real GM account (email and password) on the app's GM allowlist; there's no shared code. Ask whoever runs your app install to add you.

What Stays at the Table

The app tracks every number in these books. These are judgment calls it leaves to you:

  • Targets, flair and highlights: the app doesn't roll checks; you set the target and call the highlight.
  • Initiative order, range, cover and hazards.
  • Sub-skill effects other than Titan Chassis: the app shows the rules text and costs, you apply them.
  • Going-dark costs and story Engagement swings.
