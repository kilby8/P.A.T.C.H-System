Rewards & the Economy

Fragments buy blueprints, blueprints become permanent power, and power makes better television. Control the faucet and you control the season.

Fragment Payouts

After each encounter, the squad claims one payout:

Payout formula

12 + 9 × Spectacle + 4 × mobs defeated + 15 if all defeated + variance

Variance is −6 to +6 (the app rolls it; at the table, roll 1d12 − 6, or 2d6 − 7). Then multiply by the claiming operator's Engagement multiplier: ×1.00 below 20, +0.05 per full 20 Engagement, up to ×1.25 at 100. Round to the nearest whole fragment.

Claiming also gives that operator +3 × Spectacle Engagement (minimum 4) and +6 viewers per fragment. The squad splits the fragments however it agrees; let the most-watched operator claim it, since the multiplier is theirs.

Sector Loot

Once per encounter, or once per deployment for a stingier season, roll d100 on the sector's row to pick the blueprint tier, then pick a blueprint of that tier.

  • Weights: standard ×1, named ×0.6, legendary ×0.25.

  • The recipient gains +3 Engagement and +60 viewers.

Pick the slot that fits the story, or roll d6: 1–2 offensive, 3–4 defensive, 5–6 support. Story rewards and sponsor contracts can add more. What it works out to: over a ten-deployment season (two in each sector), each operator averages about two Tactical items and one Apex item, with the first Tactical item around mid-season and the Apex item near the finale. An Apex item should feel like an event: put it on camera.

Sponsor Drops

Any operator at 60+ Engagement is eligible. When it would make great TV (a comeback, a near-death, a perfect kill), trigger a drop: a random sponsor-grade blueprint arrives in their inbox, +12 Engagement and +450 viewers. Aim for about one drop per deployment across the squad once they're popular. Sponsor-grade blueprints are never sold and never rolled as loot.

GM Awards

Pay fragments directly for objectives, side bets and ad reads: an operator who plugs a sponsor on camera. Every award gives +2 Engagement (+8 for an ad read) and +4 viewers per fragment. The app's ad reads also offer a sponsor shield (restore a few Overshield) instead of the fee; once you pick one, the other button locks until you start a new ad read. Good sizes: 10–20 for a stunt, 30–50 for an objective, 60+ for a season moment.

Why gates matter

Destructive installation only bites when blueprints are scarce enough that every install is a choice. If operators can buy everything they want, they'll never agonize over a hardpoint. Keep the Apex tier rare: one or two Apex installs per operator per season.

Replacement Operators PLAYTEST

When an operator is destroyed, the player's new operator uses normal creation plus AdvP equal to the lowest total a surviving squadmate has earned (minimum 2), +20 fragments per completed deployment (max +200) and one Foundation blueprint of their choice; give them with the app's fragment award and blueprint inject. That keeps permadeath painful without making the new operator dead weight. Bring them in at the next Condo Phase, or mid-deployment as a "replacement contestant" if the story allows.

Advancement Pacing PLAYTEST

Give 1 AdvP per deployment survived, plus 1 for a season milestone (about every three or four deployments). A ten-deployment season then takes an operator from 2 to about 14 AdvP spent: several nodes, a handful of sub-skills and a skill rank or two. That's enough to reach tier 3 in one zone or spread across two.
