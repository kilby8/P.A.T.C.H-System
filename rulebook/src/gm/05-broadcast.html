Everything the operators do is content. Your job is to decide what it's worth.

Engagement as a Lever

Engagement is the operators' leverage over the corporation: it multiplies their pay, opens the market and summons sponsors. Most of it moves automatically (damage taken, installs, loot, payouts). Your levers are:

  • Highlights: +1 for 10+ flair on camera.
  • Ad reads: fragment awards with +8 Engagement.
  • Going-dark costs (below).
  • Story swings: a scandal at −5, a viral rescue at +5. Use these sparingly, about once a session.

Engagement has no automatic decay. If you want a "what have you done for me lately" season, take 2 off every operator at the start of each Condo Phase.PLAYTEST

Producers & Sponsors

Give the broadcast faces. A producer wants storylines and hates dead air. A sponsor rep wants their logo near a kill and their product never near a failure. A rival operator wants the same sponsor. Every one of them has an opinion about going dark.

Running Condo Scenes

One scene per player per Condo Phase, five to ten minutes each. Ask "on air or dark?" before it starts. On-air scenes may call for CLOUT + Performance (15) for Engagement; dark scenes are for plans, confessions and conspiracy clues.

The Cost of Going Dark

Going dark is never free, and its cost is never a meter. Pick one, on the spot, based on what the scene was worth to the broadcast:

Recovery & Timing

A standard Condo Phase is 3 Condo Hours: 75% of max Hardware back. Run it as a real 15-minute table break with the companion app's Condo timer (5 real minutes per Condo Hour). Shorten it with a lockdown, an emergency redeploy or a producer's whim; lengthen it for a season break. Overshield is always full on redeploy.
