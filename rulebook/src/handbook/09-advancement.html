Advancement Points

Operators grow by spending Advancement Points (AdvP). You start with 2.PLAYTEST You earn 1 AdvP for every deployment you survive, and the GM may award 1 more for a season milestone: a first Prime-Time Surge survived, a boss destroyed, a sponsor contract signed.PLAYTEST

Spend AdvP during the Condo Phase, in the app's Archetype Matrix. A node on its own grants nothing; the power is in its sub-skills, and each node has two. No refunds: only the GM can reverse a sub-skill purchase, to fix a mistake. The app applies Titan Chassis automatically (+5 max Hardware; Neural Shock at 35%).

Unlock Rules PLAYTEST

  • Tier 1 nodes are open to anyone.
  • Tier 2 needs at least one tier-1 node in the same zone.
  • Tier 3 needs at least one tier-2 node in the same zone.
  • Overlap tier 2 needs a node in both parent zones. Overlap tier 3 needs a tier-2 node in the same overlap zone.

Reading a sub-skill

Each sub-skill shows how you use it. Passive: always on, or triggers by itself. Action n AP: on your turn. Reaction n AP: off-turn, paid with saved AP. Free: no AP, with the limit shown. When a sub-skill forces an enemy save, the target is 15 (tier 1–2) or 18 (tier 3). A sub-skill that raises an attribute ("+2 CLOUT") can push it past 5.PLAYTEST
