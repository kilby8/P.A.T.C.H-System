Origins & Backgrounds

Nobody grows up wanting to be a testing platform. Everybody in the arena has a reason, and the producers know every one of them.

In this chapter

Origins

Your origin is the pool the program pulled you from. Each has its own story, its own reason for being in the arena and a shared trait every background in it gets.

Inmate Block

Cartel muscle, pit fighters, vault raiders and riot leaders, paying off a sentence one broadcast at a time. The program bought your file from the prison system. Survive enough seasons and the sentence is "served". Nobody you know has seen that happen.

Trait: Hardened

+5 maximum Hardware Integrity, and advantage on saves against being Stunned or Pinned.

Slum Distrikt Volunteer

The hungry and the would-be famous. You signed the waiver yourself, for fragments, for a shot at a sponsor, for a way out of a district the Council barely knows exists. The show is one of the only ladders up, and you intend to climb it on camera.

Trait: Premium Account

+15% starting Data Fragments (138 instead of 120). Volunteers also start with more Engagement (+8) and more viewers (+180).

Off-Grid Glitcher

Severed synths, unstable avatars, code-hunters and memory smugglers. You lived where the network's resource allocation breaks down, and something in your data never patched cleanly. The arena's hostile AI can smell it on you.

Trait: Data Anomalies

Start with a random support item: roll a d6 for your support slot, 1–3 Multi-Tool, 4–6 Field Radio. This replaces any support item from your background. Hostile AI prioritizes you: when a machine or network entity could target you or someone else equally, it chooses you. Glitchers start with +5 Engagement and +90 viewers.

Off-Worlder PLAYTEST

Pulled from a planet outside the Federation. Either you were conscripted, dragged in against your will, or you took a deal without knowing what it was. You play to get home or to earn Federation citizenship; pick one, and tell the GM which.

Trait: Unregistered (draft)

Choose any background below and use its focus, skills, loadout and blueprints (a Glitcher background keeps its own support item; no roll), but take this trait instead of that origin's. +1 skill rank at creation (rank 2 cap still applies). Once per deployment, one Federation identity system (an ID scan, a Council record, a corporate lookup) finds nothing on you. Start with 120 fragments and no Engagement or viewer bonus.

The Fifteen Backgrounds

Each background lists its focus attribute, its two trained skills (rank 2), its loadout (one item per gear slot; an empty slot takes the default Weighted Club, Thick Hide Tunic or Multi-Tool) and its starting blueprints.
