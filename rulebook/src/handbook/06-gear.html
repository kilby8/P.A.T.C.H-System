The Loadout

An operator carries three gear slots: one offensive item, one defensive item and one support item. An empty slot is filled with the default Weighted Club, Thick Hide Tunic or Multi-Tool.

Gear comes in three tiers, and the tier sets how many hardpoints (blueprint sockets) an item has:

TierHardpointsOffensiveDefensiveSupport
Foundation1Weighted Club, Iron Spear, Recurve BowThick Hide TunicMulti-Tool
Tactical2Tactical CarbineKevlar VestField Radio
Apex3Hard-Light LanceKinetic ShieldingSignal Jammer

An Apex item is three permanent installs, a small build inside your build. That makes getting one the biggest moment in an operator's economy.

New items come from the GM: sector rewards, sponsor deals and story. The terminal market sells blueprints, not items.PLAYTEST You swap items during the Condo Phase. A swapped-out item goes to your locker with its blueprints still installed, and you can swap it back later.PLAYTEST

Blueprints

A blueprint is an R&D schematic that installs into a hardpoint. Each blueprint fits one gear slot type (offensive, defensive or support) and has a tier. A blueprint of any tier fits any item of the right type.

Destructive Installation

The x-factor

Installing a blueprint consumes it. Once merged, it can't be removed, salvaged or reclaimed.

  • Install during the Condo Phase, at your terminal.PLAYTEST
  • The blueprint must fit the item's slot type, and its tier must be unlocked (the Apex tier starts locked; see the market).
  • Each install uses one copy from your stockpile.
  • Installing into an occupied hardpoint overwrites it, and the old blueprint is destroyed.
  • Installs are good television: +5 Engagement and +80 viewers, or +14 and +320 for a sponsor-grade blueprint.

The Terminal Market

During the Condo Phase you can buy blueprints with Data Fragments.

Stock is per blueprint and shared by the whole squad. Named, legendary and sponsor-grade blueprints are never sold. Each blueprint you gain, bought or looted, adds +3 Engagement and +60 viewers.

Trading

Blueprint stacks are tradable unless marked otherwise. Squadmates can hand tradable copies to each other freely between deployments.
