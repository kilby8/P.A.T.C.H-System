Engagement

Engagement is your live audience rating, from 0 to 100. It isn't a score you win. It's leverage:

  • It multiplies your fragment payouts after every encounter.
  • It opens the market: Tactical blueprints need 35, Apex blueprints 65.
  • At 60 or more, the producers can route you a sponsor drop.

Viewers is how many people are watching your feed right now. It's mostly color, the number the GM reads out when you do something stupid and glorious, but sponsors and stories notice it.

Sponsor Drops

When your Engagement is 60 or higher, the GM (playing the show's producers) can trigger a sponsor drop: a random sponsor-grade blueprint lands in your sponsor inbox, often mid-deployment. Sponsor-grade blueprints are flashy, carry a stream trait and can't be bought. Your inbox holds the 12 most recent arrivals.

Example

Rook takes 9 Overshield damage and 4 Hardware damage diving across a firing lane: +9 and +8 Engagement, pushing her from 51 to 68. Two rounds later a Holographic Cloak arrives in her inbox, with a sponsor jingle over the arena speakers.

Going Dark

In the arena you're always live. In the condo, you choose, scene by scene, whether the cameras follow you (Ch. 8). Going dark protects your privacy and your plans. It also costs you: lost Engagement, a sponsor cooling on you, a producer who starts asking questions. The GM sets the cost on the spot, the way they'd set a target.
