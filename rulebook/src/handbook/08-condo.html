The Condo Phase

Rest is an authorized maintenance interval, not a vacation benefit. All recovery windows are recorded for performance audit.

The Tower

When you jack out, you go home to a corporate condo tower that houses all of the megacorp's operators. Everyone gets a private unit; the gym, the canteen, the rooftop and the green room are shared. Every unit has a terminal. The terminal remembers everything.

The Condo Phase is the game's downtime: heal, shop, install, advance, and live a little, maybe on camera.

Running a Condo Phase

  1. Jack out. Overshield is full. Stabilized Flatlined operators wake with 1 Hardware Integrity.
  2. Recover through Condo Hours (below).
  3. Work the terminal: claim payouts, buy blueprints, trade, install, and swap items.
  4. Advance: spend Advancement Points (Ch. 9).
  5. Condo scenes: each player gets a scene, on air or dark.
  6. Redeploy.

Condo Hours & Recovery

Time in the condo is measured in Condo Hours. Each Condo Hour restores 25% of your maximum Hardware Integrity. A standard Condo Phase lasts 3 Condo Hours, which is 75% of your max.

Table break = condo time

The companion app's Condo terminal turns a real table break into recovery: 5 real minutes = 1 Condo Hour, and a 15-minute Table Break = 3 Condo Hours. Start the timer, get snacks, come back healed. The GM can lengthen or cut short a Condo Phase for story reasons; a lockdown that ends after 1 hour is 25%.

Condo Scenes

Each player gets one scene during the phase: a training session, a meeting with a sponsor rep, a fight in the canteen, a quiet call home. Before it starts, the player decides whether it's on air or dark.

On air

The feed is live. Teams can choose to live together on camera. Play to the audience and the GM may call for CLOUT + Performance against 15: success earns +2 Engagement, +1 more per full 5 flair; failure costs 1 Engagement.PLAYTEST

Dark

Cameras off. Solo operators can stay completely private, and dark scenes are where plans get made. Going dark has a cost the GM sets on the spot: lost Engagement, a cooling sponsor, a terminal that suddenly needs a "maintenance visit". There's no meter for it; the GM rules, as they would a target.
