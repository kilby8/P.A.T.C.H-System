Making a Check

When the outcome of an action is uncertain and interesting, the GM calls for a check:

  1. The GM names the attribute, the skill and the target. "PING + Stealth, target 15."
  2. Build your pool: one d6 per point of the attribute, plus one per rank of the skill. Untrained? Roll the attribute alone.
  3. Apply modifiers: advantage or disadvantage, extra dice from abilities, flat bonuses.
  4. Roll and total the dice. Meet or beat the target and you succeed.
  5. Count the flair (total minus target) on a success. Count the shortfall on a failure.
Example

Kade, a Cyber-Syndicate Tech with DATA 4 and Hacking 2, cracks a sponsor vault: DATA + Hacking against 20. Kade rolls 6d6 for 23: success with 3 flair. The vault opens, but the GM says the alarm trips a beat late; it's clean, not spectacular.

Targets are fixed. They don't scale with your pool, your level or the sector. That's the point: a bigger pool is a reliably better operator. The ladder is a playtest starting point and may be retuned after real sessions.

Flair

Every point you roll over the target is flair. Flair is how good the success looks. The GM uses it to color the narration, and some rules spend it:

  • Attacks: +1 damage for every full 5 flair.PLAYTEST
  • Highlight: 10 or more flair on a check the cameras can see earns +1 Engagement, at the GM's call.PLAYTEST

Failure has a shortfall: miss by 1 or 2 and the GM may offer success at a cost; miss by 10 and it goes wrong on camera.

Skills

Skills have ranks from 0 (untrained) to 5. Each skill has a usual attribute, but the GM may pair a skill with a different attribute when the fiction calls for it: POWER + Intimidation to loom, CLOUT + Intimidation to threaten on air.PLAYTEST

Advantage & Disadvantage PLAYTEST

Advantage: roll one extra die, then drop the lowest die. Disadvantage: roll one extra die, then drop the highest. They don't stack; if you have both, they cancel. Advantage can't turn a sure failure into success, but it lifts your odds noticeably.

Extra dice ("+1 die", "+2 dice") add to your pool and do stack. Flat bonuses ("+2 on attack rolls", "+1 to POWER checks") add to the total.

Saves

A save resists something done to you: a shockwave, a stun pulse, an intimidating warlord. Roll the named attribute plus your best related skill against the save target the effect lists.PLAYTEST "Make a POWER save (13)" means roll POWER + Athletics, say, and hit 13.

Contested Checks

When two characters act against each other (a sneak against a sentry, a grapple against a brute), both roll and the higher total wins. On a tie, the status quo holds: the sentry doesn't notice yet, the grapple doesn't break.

Helping & Group Checks

Helping: if you can plausibly assist, the helper gives the roller +1 die. Only one helper counts.PLAYTEST Group checks: when the whole squad sneaks or climbs, everyone rolls; the group succeeds if at least half succeed.
