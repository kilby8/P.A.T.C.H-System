A · Conditions

Conditions last until their stated end or until something removes them. A condition doesn't stack with itself. "Shake it off" is the 1-AP action that ends some of them with the listed check.

B · Glossary

Advantage / disadvantage p. {{pg:advantage}}
Roll one extra die, then drop the lowest (advantage) or the highest (disadvantage).
Advancement Points (AdvP) p. {{pg:advancement-points}}
Spent on nodes, sub-skills, skill ranks and attributes.
AP (Active Processing) p. {{pg:your-turn}}
Action points; gain 1 + SYSTEM ÷ 2 each turn and bank up to max + 1.
Grapple p. {{pg:actions}}
1 AP, POWER + Athletics against a POWER save; the winner pins.
Apex p. {{pg:loadout}}
Top gear tier: 3 hardpoints. The Apex market starts locked.
Archetype node p. {{pg:tree-zones}}
One of 39 entries on the tree; unlocks two sub-skills.
Arena Credits (AC)
The broadcast's name for Data Fragments; "AC Drain" and "AC Recovery" mean fragment theft and fragment loot.
Attribute bonus p. {{pg:attributes}}
Attribute − 3 (−2 to +2), added to damage.
Blueprint p. {{pg:blueprints}}
An R&D schematic installed into a hardpoint; consumed when installed.
Check p. {{pg:making-a-check}}
A pool of attribute + skill d6 against a target.
Condo Hour p. {{pg:condo-hours}}
Unit of downtime; restores 25% of max Hardware.
Counter p. {{pg:reactions}}
Armor reaction when a melee attack misses you.
Crash save p. {{pg:crash-saves}}
1d6 each turn while Flatlined; 4+ succeeds.
Data Fragments p. {{pg:market}}
Currency for the terminal market.
Defense p. {{pg:derived}}
10 + PING + armor; enemies' target to hit you.
Destructive installation p. {{pg:installation}}
Installing consumes the blueprint; there's no reclaiming it.
Engagement p. {{pg:engagement}}
Live audience rating, 0–100. Multiplies payouts and gates the market and sponsor drops.
Flair p. {{pg:flair}}
Points rolled over a target; better television.
Heat p. {{pg:node-g10}}
A scene's alert level (Calm, Alert, Lockdown); also called threat level.
Hardpoint p. {{pg:loadout}}
A blueprint socket on a gear item: 1, 2 or 3 by tier.
Hardware Integrity p. {{pg:damage}}
Your chassis; second damage layer, slow to heal.
NPC attitude p. {{pg:node-gs2}}
Hostile, Neutral or Friendly.
Overshield p. {{pg:damage}}
Digital defense layer; takes damage first and resets after each encounter.
Patch Up p. {{pg:patch-up}}
2 AP, SYSTEM + Medicine 15: heal 1d6 Hardware.
Physical damage p. {{pg:damage}}
Blunt, piercing and kinetic. Energy, heat, signal, disruption and utility are non-physical.
Pool p. {{pg:making-a-check}}
The d6 you roll: attribute + skill ranks, plus any extra dice.
Prone p. {{pg:cond-prone}}
Knocked down; see Conditions.
Save p. {{pg:saves}}
Attribute + best related skill, against an effect's save target.
Spectacle Score p. {{pg:prime-time}}
1–6; how big the broadcast wants the encounter.
Stealthed p. {{pg:cond-stealthed}}
Hidden; can't be targeted.
Sponsor drop p. {{pg:sponsor-drops}}
A sponsor-grade blueprint routed to you at 60+ Engagement.
Target p. {{pg:targets}}
Fixed difficulty: 10, 15, 20, 25 or 30.
Tier p. {{pg:loadout}}
Gear and blueprint grade: Foundation, Tactical or Apex (the app calls it "theme").

C · Playtest Register

Rules marked PLAYTEST fill gaps in the design with a rule modeled on the matching fifth-edition rule, adapted to the d6 pool. They're the defaults until the table decides otherwise. Everything else in this book matches the companion app.
