Building an Operator

Eight steps from a name on an intake form to a contestant on the feed. Bring a pencil; the terminal will want everything in triplicate.

Creation at a Glance

  1. Pick an origin. Inmate Block, Slum Distrikt Volunteer, Off-Grid Glitcher or (playtest) Off-Worlder. Your origin gives a shared trait and your reason for being in the arena (Ch. 3).
  2. Pick a background from that origin. It sets your focus attribute, two trained skills and your starter loadout.
  3. Assign CPU attributes. Every attribute starts at 1. Distribute 12 points; no attribute may go above 4 at creation.
  4. Record skills. Your two background skills start at rank 2. Add 2 more ranks anywhere, to a maximum of rank 2 at creation.PLAYTEST
  5. Work out derived stats: Hardware Integrity, Overshield, AP and Defense (table below).
  6. Take your loadout and blueprints from your background (Ch. 6). Starting blueprints go to your stockpile; you may install them now, as if in a Condo Phase.
  7. Note your broadcast numbers: Data Fragments, Engagement and viewer count.
  8. Spend 2 Advancement Points on the archetype tree, usually one tier-1 node and one of its sub-skills (Ch. 9).PLAYTEST

Finish with a name, a look and one sentence about why you're in the arena. The GM will use that sentence against you.

The Six CPU Attributes

Inside the arena your body is a processor running a combat build. Six attributes describe its specs. Each runs from 1 to 5; 3 is an average operator.

AttributeCoversFeeds
POWERRaw force: lifting, striking, grappling, intimidatingMelee attacks & damage
PINGReflexes, speed, aim, stealth, fine controlRanged attacks, Defense, initiative
HARDWAREChassis integrity, trauma resistance, staminaHardware Integrity, heavy & energy weapons
DATAInformation, hacking, shields, techOvershield, support gear
SYSTEMMental processing, perception, composureAP, Overshield
CLOUTPresence, audience appeal, broadcast pullEngagement, viewers

Twelve points over a base of 1 gives an average of 3 in every attribute. Spreading evenly makes a generalist. Pushing two attributes to 4 makes a specialist with holes. Your background's focus attribute is a recommendation, not a rule, but its gear and skills are built around it.

Some damage and broadcast math uses an attribute's bonus: the attribute minus 3, giving −2 at 1 up to +2 at 5.

Hardware Integrity

5 + 5 × HARDWARE. Your biological chassis. Damage reaches it only after your Overshield is gone, and it heals slowly. Inmates add +5 (the Hardened trait).

Overshield

16 + 2 × SYSTEM + DATA. Your digital defense layer, from 19 to 31. It soaks damage first and comes back fully after every encounter.

Active Processing (AP)

1 + SYSTEM ÷ 2, rounded down. The action points you get at the start of each of your turns. Neural Shock costs you 1 of them.

Defense PLAYTEST

10 + PING + armor bonus. The target number enemies roll against to hit you. Armor gives +1 (Thick Hide Tunic), +2 (Kevlar Vest) or +3 (Kinetic Shielding).

Speed

Every operator moves 6 meters per Move.

Worked Example: Rook

Building Rook, step by step

1–2. Origin Slum Distrikt Volunteer, background The Neon Runner (focus PING; skills Acrobatics and Survival).

3. Base 1 everywhere, then 12 points: POWER +1, PING +3, HARDWARE +2, DATA +1, SYSTEM +3, CLOUT +2. Final: POWER 2, PING 4, HARDWARE 3, DATA 2, SYSTEM 4, CLOUT 3.

4. Acrobatics 2, Survival 2, and both free ranks into Marksmanship (rank 2).

5. Hardware Integrity 5 + 15 = 20. Overshield 16 + 8 + 2 = 26. AP 1 + 2 = 3. Defense 10 + 4 + 1 (Thick Hide Tunic) = 15.

6. Recurve Bow, Field Radio, plus the default Thick Hide Tunic in the empty defensive slot. Blueprints: Precision Sight, Signal Booster, both kept in the stockpile while Rook holds out for a better weapon.

7. 138 Data Fragments. Engagement 25 + 0 + 8 = 33. Viewers 120 + 180 + 180 = 480.

8. Unlocks G2 Neural Reflex (1 point) and its sub-skill Reflex Dodge (1 point).

Rook's bow attack rolls PING 4 + Marksmanship 2 = 6d6 and deals 1d8+1 kinetic damage.
