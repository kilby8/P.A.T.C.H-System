Reading a Stat Block

  • Header: name and template number (the app's encounter ID).
  • Role · tier · kind: the app's role keyword, its threat tier, and what it is. "Machine" and "network entity" count as hostile AI (they target Glitchers first) and are valid Field Radio targets.
  • Integrity / AP: exactly what the app spawns.
  • Defense: operators' target to hit it. Init: its fixed initiative. Threat: its cost in the encounter budget.
  • Attack: roll the dice plus the bonus against an operator's Defense. Saves is the pool it rolls to resist operator abilities; its own effects use the listed save target.
  • Abilities cost AP when they say so; otherwise they're always on.
Playtest numbers

Integrity, AP, roles and descriptions come straight from the companion app. Defense, attack, damage, Init, threat and abilities are PLAYTEST values built from the threat-tier table, modeled on fifth-edition stat blocks and tuned to the d6 pool.

Threat Tiers

The companion app's list has 50 entries, but three (#24, #42, #43) duplicate another entry, so this book has 47 stat blocks. Same-name variants are labeled, as in "Mainframe-Guardian (Monolith)". A few entities break their tier template (the Pixel-Hound has 2 AP); the stat block wins. "AC" in a role name means Arena Credits, the broadcast's word for Data Fragments.

Budget 4 threat per operator for a solid fight (Game Master's Guide, Chapter 3). Promote any entity one tier for a named rival or a finale: +8 Integrity, +1 AP, +1 attack die, +2 Defense, +2 threat.
